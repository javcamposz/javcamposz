## Selected Research

### External review of a frontier AI safety case

**[Lessons from External Review of DeepMind's Scheming Inability Safety Case](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21964)**, presented as an oral at the Second Workshop on Technical AI Governance Research, ICML 2026.

We applied Assurance 2.0 to Google DeepMind's public safety case for Gemini 2.5 Pro, identified concerns affecting its scope and use in deployment decisions, and proposed a practical process for independent review.

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21964) · [ICML oral and video](https://icml.cc/virtual/2026/78598) · [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=WFwP3PoUCC)

### AgentMisalignment

**[AgentMisalignment: Measuring the Propensity for Misaligned Behaviour in LLM-Based Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04018)** introduces realistic agent environments for evaluating shutdown resistance, oversight evasion, sandbagging, deception, and power-seeking.

Our experiments found that system-prompted personality can change misalignment propensity as much as, and sometimes more than, model choice. I co-developed the benchmark through the Cambridge AI Safety Hub.

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04018) · [Benchmark implementation](https://github.com/javcamposz/inspect_evals/tree/main/src/inspect_evals/agentmisalignment)
